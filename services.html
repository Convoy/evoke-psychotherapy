Services

Three ways to work together.

Therapy for adults and couples navigating hard seasons, and supervision for clinicians building a career.

01 — For clients

Individual therapy

Ongoing weekly work for adults. We start with what brought you in and build from there. No intake questionnaire will ever tell the whole story.

Common reasons people come in: anxiety that will not turn off, the aftereffects of trauma, depression, grief, a relationship or career that has shifted underneath them, or the particular exhaustion of caring for others professionally.

02 — For couples

Couples therapy

Work for partners who want to understand the pattern they keep landing in rather than relitigate the last argument. We slow the cycle down enough to see what each of you is protecting.

Common reasons couples come in: recurring conflict, distance or disconnection, a breach of trust, and the strain that demanding careers and caregiving put on a relationship.

03 — For clinicians

Clinical supervision

Supervision for Limited Licensed Counselors accruing hours toward full licensure in Michigan. Case consultation, ethics, documentation, and the parts of the work that training programs tend to skip.

Supervision here is developmental rather than evaluative. We look at your cases, but also at how you are holding them, which is usually the harder question early in a career.

Rates & insurance

Cost should not be the reason someone stops looking. If none of these fit, say so and we will work something out.

Insurance

Several major Michigan plans are accepted. Reach out with your carrier and we will confirm coverage before your first session.

Private pay

Due at the time of service. Card, HSA, and FSA accepted. A superbill is available for out-of-network reimbursement.

Sliding scale

A limited number of reduced-fee spots are held open. Ask during your consult.
