Growing the practice

We are hiring therapists.

evoke psychotherapy is adding clinicians who want a thoughtful caseload and real support. Fully licensed therapists and limited licensed counselors are both welcome to reach out.

Positions available

Full time

A full caseload with benefits, supervision, and a room of your own.

Part time

A smaller caseload on the days that work around the rest of your life.

Hybrid

Some days in the Kalamazoo office, the rest of your week from home.

Virtual

Fully remote telehealth, anywhere you are licensed in Michigan.

What we provide

Supervision included

Board-approved supervision toward full licensure, in house and on the schedule.

A real office

Furnished rooms downtown, plus the telehealth platform and billing already in place.

Referrals that fit

Clients matched to your training and interests rather than to whoever has an opening.
